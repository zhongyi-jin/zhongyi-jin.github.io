About

I am currently a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Aeronautical and Aviation Engineering at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University. I received my PhD from The Hong Kong Polytechnic University under the supervision of Prof. Kam Hung Ng. During my doctoral studies, I was a Visiting Doctoral Researcher in the Department of Decision Sciences at HEC Montréal, supervised by Prof. Okan Arslan.

I hold a Master's Degree from Dalian University of Technology in Port, Coastal, and Offshore Engineering and a Bachelor's Degree in Port, Waterway, and Coastal Engineering from Ocean University of China. I will join HEC Montréal as a Postdoctoral Fellow, working with Prof. Okan Arslan and Prof. Maryam Daryalal this year, supported by the GERAD Postdoctoral Fellowship Competition 2026-2027.

My research interests center on low-altitude air mobility and logistics systems, as well as airport and seaport operations management. I have authored over ten papers in peer-reviewed journals such as Transportation Research Part A/D/E, International Journal of Production Research, and Omega. I also actively contribute to the academic community as a reviewer for more than twenty journals, such as Transportation Research Part A/C/D/E, Computers & Operations Research, International Journal of Production Research, and Transport Policy.
