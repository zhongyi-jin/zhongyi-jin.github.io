About

Dr. Zhongyi Jin is a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Aeronautical and Aviation Engineering at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University. In October 2026, he will join HEC Montreal as a Postdoctoral Fellow supported by the GERAD Postdoctoral Fellowship Competition 2026-2027.

His work lies at the intersection of operations research, data-driven optimisation, and transport economics. He develops decision models for low-altitude air mobility, airport and seaport operations, and transportation and logistics systems under uncertainty.
