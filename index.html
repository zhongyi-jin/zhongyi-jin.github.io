About

Dr. Zhongyi Jin is currently a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Aeronautical and Aviation Engineering at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University. He received his PhD in Aeronautical and Aviation Engineering under the supervision of Prof. Kam Hung Ng. During his doctoral studies, he was a Visiting Doctoral Researcher in the Department of Decision Sciences at HEC Montreal, supervised by Prof. Okan Arslan.

He holds a Master of Engineering from Dalian University of Technology and a Bachelor of Engineering from Ocean University of China. In October 2026, he will join HEC Montreal as a Postdoctoral Fellow, working with Prof. Okan Arslan and Prof. Maryam Daryalal, supported by the GERAD Postdoctoral Fellowship Competition 2026-2027.

His research lies at the intersection of operations research, data-driven optimisation, and transport economics. He serves as a reviewer for international journals including Advanced Engineering Informatics, Computers & Industrial Engineering, Journal of Air Transport Management, Transportation Research Part E, and International Journal of Production Research.
